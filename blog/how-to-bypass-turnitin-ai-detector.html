Why Does Turnitin Flag Essays?

Turnitin looks for statistical anomalies in writing. It measures "burstiness" (how much your sentence lengths vary) and "perplexity" (how predictable your vocabulary is). When students write structured academic papers, they often use uniform sentence lengths and formal vocabulary—which happens to perfectly mimic how ChatGPT writes.
