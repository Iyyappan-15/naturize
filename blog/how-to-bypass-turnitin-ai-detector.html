How to Make Your Essays Score Better on Turnitin's AI Detector in 2026

Last updated: May 2026

Universities across the United States, UK, India, and Canada are increasingly relying on Turnitin's AI detection feature to flag student submissions. While this is meant to catch purely AI-generated work, it often produces false positives — flagging well-written, structured essays as "AI-like" even when a student wrote them independently.

This guide explains why that happens and how you can make your writing sound clearer, more natural, and less robotic — so it genuinely reflects your own voice.

Why Does Turnitin Flag Essays?

Turnitin's detector measures two key linguistic properties:

  • Burstiness — how much your sentence lengths vary. AI text is very uniform. Humans naturally mix short punchy sentences with longer explanatory ones.
  • Perplexity — how predictable your word choices are. AI tends to always pick the most "expected" next word. Humans surprise readers with specific details, opinions, and personal expressions.

When your writing looks too uniform and too predictable, Turnitin flags it — even if you wrote every word yourself.
