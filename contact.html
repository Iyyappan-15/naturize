Common Questions

Why is the humanizer not working?
Make sure the app is deployed on Vercel with the GROQ_API_KEY environment variable set. Opening index.html directly as a file will not work — the API endpoints require a server.
Can I use Naturize for free forever?
Yes! The free tier gives you 3 humanizations and 3 detections every day, and it resets automatically at midnight. No credit card needed.
Does Naturize store my text?
No. Your text is sent to Groq AI for processing and immediately discarded. We don't log or store any of the content you paste. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
Will humanized text always pass AI detectors?
Naturize significantly reduces AI-like patterns in text, but no tool can guarantee 100% results across all detectors. Detection technology is constantly evolving. Always review the output yourself.
