# Idea Brainstorming: What to Build Next

You are totally right. Building an ATS resume checker is pointless because Claude/ChatGPT already do it perfectly. We need to build tools that **solve a highly specific emotional problem** or **do something ChatGPT refuses or fails to do naturally.**

Here are 4 awesome, unconventional ideas that fit the Naturize brand (making AI sound human) and have high viral potential.

### Idea 1: The "Corporate Translator" (Passive-Aggressive to Professional)
People love tools that are slightly funny but actually useful. 
*   **The Problem:** People get angry at work and write emotional or passive-aggressive drafts, but they know they can't send them.
*   **How it works:** They paste their angry, unfiltered thought (e.g., *"I already told you this yesterday, are you blind?"*). The tool translates it into a perfectly polite, humanized corporate email (*"Just following up on my note from yesterday regarding..."*).
*   **Viral Angle:** It's highly shareable on TikTok/Reels ("How I reply to my boss using Naturize").

### Idea 2: The "Reddit & Twitter Reply Humanizer" (Engagement Farmer)
*   **The Problem:** People use AI to write replies to grow their Twitter or Reddit accounts, but AI sounds incredibly fake (*"Ah, this is a profound perspective..."*). Redditors instantly downvote AI.
*   **How it works:** You paste the original post. The tool generates a witty, contrarian, or deeply insightful reply in a casual, slightly cynical "internet tone." It uses lowercase, no periods at the end of sentences, and internet slang.
*   **Monetization Angle:** Social media managers and ghostwriters will pay for this.

### Idea 3: The "High-Stakes Apology Generator"
*   **The Problem:** Writing an apology to a client, boss, or angry customer is stressful. If you use ChatGPT, it sounds robotic and insincere, which makes the person even angrier.
*   **How it works:** The user enters what they messed up. The tool uses psychological principles to write a deeply empathetic, non-defensive apology that takes extreme ownership. It sounds like a real human taking accountability.
*   **Why it works:** People are desperate when they need an apology. They need it to sound perfect.

### Idea 4: The "Cold Email De-Cringer"
*   **The Problem:** 99% of cold sales emails are obviously written by AI (*"I hope this finds you well", "synergistic growth"*).
*   **How it works:** Sales reps paste their terrible AI draft. The tool aggressively strips out the "cringe" and rewrites it to sound like a quick 2-sentence note typed on an iPhone from a peer. 
*   **Monetization Angle:** B2B Sales teams have huge budgets and will pay for a tool that increases their reply rates.
